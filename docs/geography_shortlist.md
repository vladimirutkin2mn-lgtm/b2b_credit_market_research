# Geography Shortlist — First Research Wave

Version date: 2026-10-02.

## Objective

Select the first markets for the SME / B2B lending landscape before choosing individual lenders for deep dives.

The shortlist is designed for **analytical coverage**, not as a ranking of countries.

We want a set that jointly covers:

- large and economically relevant SME-credit systems;
- bank-led and non-bank/challenger-led models;
- mature and emerging markets;
- relationship lending and highly automated lending;
- strong public-data environments;
- different credit/data infrastructures;
- different regulatory and capital regimes.

## Selection criteria

Each market was screened on five internal research-priority dimensions:

1. **Scale / strategic relevance** — size and relevance of the SME/business-credit system.
2. **Data availability** — public statistics, borrower surveys, lender disclosures and source quality.
3. **Digital / alternative-lending relevance** — availability of digital banks, fintech lenders, embedded or data-rich credit models.
4. **Archetype diversity** — ability to study materially different lender models in one market.
5. **Cross-market learning value** — usefulness for testing transferable hypotheses.

# Why each market is in scope

## United States

### Why it matters
- Very broad lender universe: large banks, regional/community banks, fintech lenders, merchant/payment lenders, SBA-backed lending and embedded credit.
- Strong borrower-side evidence through the Federal Reserve Banks’ Small Business Credit Survey.
- Strong regulatory and bank-level disclosure environment.

### Key public-data anchor
Federal Reserve Banks — Small Business Credit Survey:
https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey

The 2026 employer-firm report covers 6,525 small employer firms and defines the SBCS population as firms with fewer than 500 employees.

### Research value
Excellent baseline for:
- approval and application behavior;
- bank vs fintech lender choice;
- satisfaction and friction;
- relationship vs new-to-lender lending;
- bank economics and regulatory disclosures.

### Definition warning
There is **no single universal US SME definition**. SBA size standards vary by NAICS industry and may be based on receipts or employees. Survey populations often use <500 employees.

## United Kingdom

### Why it matters
- Mature challenger/specialist-bank and non-bank lending ecosystem.
- Strong public borrower-finance research.
- Useful open-banking environment for studying data substitution and digital journeys.
- Good mix of incumbents, challenger banks and specialist lenders.

### Key public-data anchor
British Business Bank — Small Business Finance Markets 2026:
https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/about/research-and-publications/small-business-finance-markets-report-2026

The report tracks lending-market structure, borrower demand and finance usage. It reports that challenger and specialist banks have materially increased their share of gross SME bank lending over time.

### Research value
Strong market to test:
- whether challenger models structurally improve SME access;
- digital application and open-banking journeys;
- product fragmentation beyond term loans;
- incumbent vs specialist economics.

### Definition warning
UK statistical, procurement and lender definitions differ. Keep original source definition.

## Brazil

### Why it matters
- Strong digital-bank / fintech ecosystem.
- Particularly relevant to the project because of Nubank and other digital credit models.
- Banco Central do Brasil provides unusually granular credit data by company size, product and delinquency.
- Open Finance provides a useful data-sharing context.

### Key public-data anchors
Banco Central do Brasil — credit by company size:
https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/27701-saldo-das-operacoes-de-credito-por-porte-da-empresa---micro-pequena-e-media-mpme

SCR.data:
https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/scrdata

BCB publishes monthly aggregated SCR measures including active portfolio and delinquency with cuts by legal-person/customer type, credit modality, geography, industry and client size.

### Research value
Very strong market for testing:
- transaction-data / ecosystem underwriting;
- digital-bank SME expansion;
- high-frequency portfolio-risk data;
- incumbent vs fintech/customer-base advantages.

### Definition warning
Brazil has materially different legal/tax and credit-reporting size definitions. For credit research, BCB/SCR borrower-size classification is especially important.

## Germany

### Why it matters
- Large relationship-banking and Mittelstand market.
- Strong contrast to highly automated micro-SME models.
- KfW provides a uniquely deep longitudinal SME financing dataset.
- Useful for studying larger-ticket SME and relationship-manager underwriting.

### Key public-data anchor
KfW SME Panel:
https://www.kfw.de/About-KfW/KfW-Research/KfW-Mittelstandspanel.html

KfW states that the Panel has been conducted since 2003 and covers private enterprises with annual turnover up to €500 million, with up to 15,000 companies per year.

### Research value
Strong market for:
- medium/upper-SME financing;
- relationship lending;
- investment credit;
- government-supported finance;
- comparison between EU SME definition and broader “Mittelstand”.

### Definition warning
KfW’s core SME/Mittelstand research population can be far broader than the EU SME threshold. Never mix them without relabeling.

## India

### Why it matters
- Very large MSME population and bank-credit system.
- Formal priority-sector treatment creates distinctive credit economics and incentives.
- Strong digital-data infrastructure including Udyam and Account Aggregator.
- Useful lender archetypes: public-sector banks, private banks, small finance banks, NBFCs and fintechs.

### Key public-data anchors
Reserve Bank of India — Annual Report / Bank Credit to MSMEs:
https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx

Department of Financial Services — Account Aggregator Framework:
https://www.financialservices.gov.in/account-aggregator-framework

### Research value
Strong market for testing:
- cash-flow-based lending;
- consented financial-data sharing;
- priority-sector effects;
- collateral-free / guarantee-supported lending;
- bank–fintech partnerships.

### Definition warning
MSME thresholds were expanded effective 1 April 2025. Historical series must be checked for classification breaks.

---

## Netherlands

### Why it matters
- EU-comparable SME definition and regulatory environment.
- Highly digital banking environment.
- New DNB SME-specific lending statistics improve market comparability.
- Useful market for incumbent digital journeys and alternative SME-finance providers.

### Key public-data anchor
De Nederlandsche Bank — SME corporate lending:
https://www.dnb.nl/en/general-news/statistical-news/2026/almost-half-of-corporate-lending-goes-to-smes-interest-rates-slightly-higher/

In May 2026 DNB began presenting SME-specific corporate-lending figures, including SME share and interest-rate differences.

### Research value
Good EU laboratory for:
- open-banking-enabled flows;
- digital incumbent SME lending;
- pricing differences by size;
- comparison with Germany under the same EU policy definition.

---

## Australia

### Why it matters
- Strong, clean central-bank statistics on business lending rates split by small, medium and large businesses.
- Diverse bank and non-bank SME lender set.
- RBA explicitly discusses automation and competition in business credit.
- Clear ABS business-size statistical bands.

### Key public-data anchors
RBA — business lending rates:
https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/interest-rates/

ABS — business characteristics / size:
https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/technology-and-innovation/characteristics-australian-business/latest-release

### Research value
Useful for:
- pricing by borrower size;
- bank competition;
- automated approval;
- secured vs unsecured SME lending;
- clean comparison of small and medium business rate levels.

### Definition warning
Australia uses multiple definitions by policy context. ABS statistical size bands are employee-based; tax definitions can be turnover-based.

---

## Mexico

### Why it matters
- Strong emerging-market contrast to US/Europe.
- Excellent enterprise-financing demand survey through INEGI/CNBV.
- Sector-specific official MIPYME size definitions.
- Useful mix of banks, fintechs and alternative finance.

### Key public-data anchor
INEGI / CNBV — ENAFIN 2024:
https://en.www.inegi.org.mx/programas/enafin/2024/

ENAFIN captures financing needs, sources, access conditions, credit applications and use of financial services by private non-financial enterprises.

### Research value
Useful for:
- access-to-finance constraints;
- bank vs alternative finance;
- application and rejection behavior;
- emerging-market data/friction comparison with Brazil and India.

### Definition warning
The official MIPYME definition combines employment, annual sales and sector. ENAFIN sampling uses employment strata and has specific micro-enterprise scope, so survey results must not be treated as covering every micro firm.
