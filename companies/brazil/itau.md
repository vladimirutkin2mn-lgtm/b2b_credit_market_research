# Company / Lender Teardown — Itaú Empresas

**Status:** Batch 2 first-pass teardown  
**Research date:** 2026-10-02  
**Geography:** Brazil  
**Primary segments:** S2/S3/S5  
**Primary question:** how does a highly profitable deposit-funded incumbent use digital distribution and government guarantees in SME credit?

> Quality standard: `docs/consulting_quality_standard.md`

---

## Executive synthesis — answer first

**Itaú's SME model shows that the incumbent advantage is a combination of balance-sheet economics, relationship data and risk-sharing — not simply cheaper funding.** Its very-small/small/middle-market book is already R$307bn, while government-guaranteed facilities are being moved into the app and growing materially faster than the rest of the portfolio. This creates a digital SME lane inside an institution with strong group profitability and capital.

### Quantified proof points

2Q26:
- very small, small and middle-market credit portfolio: **R$307.4bn**, +11.6% YoY reported / +13.2% ex-FX;
- total group credit portfolio: **R$1.522trn**;
- SME government-sponsored portfolio: **+7.2% QoQ**;
- government-sponsored SME originations: **+47.3% vs 1Q26**;
- group recurring managerial result: **R$12.4bn**;
- recurring managerial ROE: **24.3% consolidated**, 25.7% Brazil;
- group cost of credit / loan portfolio: **2.7%**;
- CET1: **12.3%**.

In 2024, digital contracting of government credit lines grew almost **50% YoY**; FGI contracts more than doubled, and within three months of app launch the app already represented 17% of annual FGI contracted volume and >30% in December.

### Mechanism

Large current-account/client base
+ deposit-funded bank
+ digital app
+ government guarantee
→ lower borrower friction
→ lower lender loss exposure / collateral constraint
→ higher eligible approval capacity
→ scalable SME originations.

### Strategic implication

Government guarantee should not be treated as just another product feature. When embedded digitally, it becomes part of the **risk architecture** that can expand approval and lower capital/loss intensity.

### Transferability

**Transferable with local guarantee infrastructure and digital tax/revenue data.**

### Counter-evidence

Most public profitability and cost-of-credit metrics are group-level. The R$307bn SME/middle-market book is public, but product-level yield, FGI loss rate and RAROC are not.

## 2. Product architecture

### Standard working-capital / business credit
Itaú Empresas offers:
- capital de giro;
- revolving/working-capital products;
- secured structures;
- larger relationship-led financing.

### FGI PEAC
Current public product:
- companies with annual revenue up to **R$300m**;
- up to **79 months**;
- fixed rate;
- BNDES guarantee complement;
- simulation/contracting available in Itaú Empresas app/computer for eligible clients.

Source:
https://www.itau.com.br/empresas/emprestimos-financiamentos/fgi

### Pronampe
For smaller companies within program eligibility:
- revenue data shared through e-CAC;
- eligible offer surfaced in Itaú Empresas;
- simulation + digital token acceptance.

The program embeds government eligibility and tax data into the journey.

## 3. Customer journey — government-backed fast lane

### Existing Itaú client

1. Open app / internet banking.
2. Navigate to installment loans / FGI or Pronampe.
3. Check offer availability.
4. Simulate amount/term.
5. Revenue/tax eligibility is checked through program data/process.
6. Accept terms digitally.
7. Contract via token/authentication.
8. Funds credited according to facility process.

### Visual evidence

Official product pages document the exact app route, but no stable first-party transaction screenshots were captured.

**Screen evidence:** process documented; key customer screens missing.

## 4. Underwriting / risk architecture

Core layers:
- ordinary bank credit analysis;
- existing relationship / transaction data;
- tax/revenue eligibility for some programs;
- government guarantee eligibility;
- product-specific financial/collateral requirements.

### Why FGI matters

The guarantee changes expected loss, and therefore potentially:
- approval threshold;
- required collateral;
- pricing;
- provision/capital economics.

The project's benchmark should therefore compare guaranteed and ordinary credit separately.

---

## 5. Risk

2Q26 public group evidence:
- group cost of credit: **R$10.1bn** for the quarter;
- cost-of-credit / loan-portfolio ratio: **2.7%**, stable;
- the quarter's credit-cost increase was driven in part by Brazilian retail-business companies as the portfolio grew.

Public charts indicate relatively low delinquency for the aggregated “very small, small and middle market” portfolio, but precise product-level NPL for FGI/Pronampe is not separately disclosed in the source used here.

### Caveat
Do not use group 2.7% cost of credit as SME cost of risk.

## 6. Economics

Group:
- financial margin with clients: **R$32.6bn** in 2Q26;
- recurring managerial result: **R$12.4bn**;
- ROE: 24.3%;
- CET1: 12.3%.

This establishes a strong incumbent economic/funding context.

It does **not** establish:

## 7. Digital adoption evidence

Itaú disclosed for 2024:
- government-line contracting through internet/app up nearly **50%** vs 2023;
- FGI agreements >2x;
- FGI app launch in October;
- app produced **17%** of full-year FGI contracting in only three months;
- >**30%** of December FGI volume.

This is rare evidence that a digital lending journey materially changed channel mix, not merely that the feature exists.

## 8. Hypothesis tests

### H1 — Existing-customer advantage: supported
App-based offers live within Itaú Empresas relationship.

### H3 — funding advantage: structurally supported
Itaú is a large deposit-funded bank with high group ROE, but SME-specific spread is not public.

### H4 — data substitution: supported
Tax/revenue information and program eligibility reduce manual-document steps in government facilities.

### H5 — SME is several businesses: strongly supported
The reported portfolio spans very small through middle market; digital government-backed products coexist with relationship-led larger exposures.

---

## 9. Strategic implication

**Guarantees can be digitized into the credit factory.**

The strategic sequence is:
program eligibility + tax/revenue data
→ automatic pre-check
→ bank risk analysis
→ guarantee lowers uncovered risk
→ offer in existing banking channel
→ digital contract.

This can increase both customer speed and risk-adjusted approval capacity.
