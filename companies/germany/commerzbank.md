# Company / Lender Teardown — Commerzbank Unternehmerkunden

**Status:** Batch 3 first-pass teardown  
**Research date:** 2026-10-02  
**Geography:** Germany  
**Primary segments:** S4/S5 — Mittelstand relationship lending  
**Primary question:** what does a modern relationship-bank fast lane look like inside a large incumbent?

> Quality standard: `docs/consulting_quality_standard.md`

---

## Executive synthesis — answer first

**Commerzbank shows that relationship lending can be made faster without pretending it is fully automated.** Its Gewerbekredit journey takes only minutes to initiate online, optional documents can be uploaded immediately, an adviser contacts the customer within 48 hours, and lines up to €100k can be decided directly in the consultation. The differentiator is a shorter path to an empowered human decision-maker.

### Quantified proof points

2025 group / Corporate Clients:
- group revenues: **€12.2bn**, +10%;
- operating result: **€4.5bn**, +18%;
- NPE ratio: **1.1%**;
- group risk result: **-€722m**;
- Corporate Clients average loan volume Q4: **€115bn**, +10%;
- Corporate Clients revenue: **€4.865bn**;
- Corporate Clients operating profit: **€2.151bn**.
- Gewerbekredit starts from **€5,000**;
- adviser contact within **48 hours**;
- up to **€100,000** can be decided directly in adviser conversation.

### Mechanism

2-minute digital intake
→ optional upfront document upload
→ rapid routing to adviser
→ empowered in-conversation decision for smaller line
→ deeper relationship underwriting for larger cases.

### Strategic implication

In medium-SME banking, reducing **organizational latency** may matter as much as automating credit analysis.

### Transferability

**High for incumbent relationship banks.**

### Counter-evidence

The Corporate Clients segment includes much more than SMEs, and group/segment financial performance cannot be attributed to Gewerbekredit. The digital front end is still followed by human contact.

## 1. Product — Gewerbekredit

Public current terms:
- revolving overdraft/current-account credit;
- from **€5k**;
- limit personalized by creditworthiness/company size;
- online request in ~**2 minutes**;
- optional upload of balance sheet / EÜR and documents;
- adviser contact within max **48 hours**;
- decision up to **€100k** directly in adviser consultation.

Source:

## 2. Customer journey

| Stage | Experience |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Gewerbekredit page |
| Application | 2-minute online form |
| Documents | optional initial upload |
| Routing | application review |
| Adviser | contact within 48 hours |
| Decision | ≤€100k directly in consultation |
| Larger/complex | further credit analysis / documentation |
| Servicing | line attached to business current account |

The journey design is not STP. It is **fast digital-to-human orchestration**.

## 7. Hypothesis tests

### H2 — speed is segmentation: strongly supported
A clear ≤€100k direct-decision boundary exists.

### H5 — SME is several businesses: strongly supported
Small revolving liquidity and broader Mittelstand credit use different decision depth.

### H4 — data substitution: weak/partial
The model speeds routing and decision rights more than it removes documentation.

### H3 — funding advantage: structurally supported
Large universal-bank funding exists, but product economics are not isolated.

---

## 8. Strategic implication

Commerzbank suggests another route to SME speed:

> **Compress handoffs and push approval authority closer to the customer.**

Not all process improvement requires predictive automation. For higher-value SME lending, empowered decision-makers can be the highest-ROI intervention.
