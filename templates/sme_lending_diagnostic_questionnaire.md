# SME Lending Diagnostic Questionnaire

**Purpose:** structured management interviews / workshop input before bank-specific opportunity sizing.

Use with:
- Head of SME
- CRO / Credit
- CFO / Finance
- Treasury
- Product
- Operations
- Data/Technology
- Relationship Banking
- Collections

---

# A. Portfolio / segmentation

1. How do you define SME internally?
2. What share of book/originations sits in each ticket band?
3. Which products materially differ in underwriting?
4. Which borrower types currently share one process but should not?
5. What share of originations is existing-customer vs new-to-bank?
6. What share of SME customers have an operating/current account?
7. What share have acquiring/payments relationship?
8. Where are the largest current growth and loss pools?

---

# B. Existing-customer lending

1. Do you calculate credit eligibility before an application?
2. What percentage of existing SMEs currently have a refreshed eligible limit?
3. How often is eligibility refreshed?
4. Which transaction features are used?
5. Can a customer see an offer before starting an application?
6. What still needs to be re-entered or re-uploaded?
7. Is pricing/limit different for existing vs new?
8. What are existing-vs-new loss rates and CAC?

---

# C. Data / documents

1. Which documents are requested by ticket/product?
2. Which data are already available internally?
3. Which documents duplicate data already held?
4. Is Open Banking used?
5. Is tax data used?
6. Is accounting data used?
7. Is acquiring data used?
8. Is invoice data used?
9. What percentage of cases require manual document handling?
10. What are the most common data-quality exceptions?

---

# D. Decisioning / risk perimeter

1. What can be approved STP today?
2. Where are ticket thresholds?
3. Where are tenor thresholds?
4. What industries/legal forms are excluded?
5. What causes escalation to human review?
6. Are thresholds evidence-based or legacy?
7. How often are rules/thresholds reviewed?
8. Do you know loss and conversion immediately above/below thresholds?
9. Are manual overrides measured?
10. Who has final authority by ticket/risk?

---

# E. Relationship lending

1. How many handoffs occur in a standard medium-SME case?
2. Who owns the case end to end?
3. What can the RM decide?
4. What can the underwriter decide?
5. How long from complete file to decision?
6. How many clarification loops occur?
7. How much analyst time is spent on data gathering vs judgment?
8. How is banker/underwriter productivity measured?
9. Is RAROC visible at decision time?
10. What creates the most customer waiting?

---

# F. Customer journey

1. Can customer see application status?
2. Can customer see missing items?
3. Can customer see expected next step?
4. Are decision time and funding time measured separately?
5. How many customer-entered fields are required?
6. How many documents?
7. What is abandonment by step?
8. What is support-contact rate by step?
9. How quickly are additional-info requests raised?
10. Are repeat customers sent through the same path?

---

# G. Repeat / servicing

1. What percentage of borrowers borrow again?
2. How long until repeat draw?
3. Is there a reusable line?
4. Are limits refreshed automatically?
5. Are top-ups proactive?
6. Are repeat economics better than first-loan economics?
7. Are repeat losses better after controlling for selection?
8. What servicing actions still require manual support?

---

# H. Guarantee programs

1. Which schemes are used?
2. Which customer segments qualify?
3. What share of approvals depend on a guarantee?
4. How much incremental approval does the guarantee create?
5. What is uncovered loss?
6. What are guarantee fees?
7. What is claim success and turnaround?
8. What is the actual capital/RWA treatment?
9. Is program eligibility integrated digitally?
10. Is guaranteed lending P&L tracked separately?

---

# I. Funding / capital

1. What FTP is applied by product?
2. Is committed-line liquidity charged differently?
3. Is capital visible at product/factory level?
4. Are there assets that could be distributed/securitised?
5. What constrains growth today: capital, funding, risk appetite, demand or operations?
6. Which products have attractive gross yield but weak ROA/RAROC?
7. Are guarantees treated as funding/risk tools in product design?
8. Is originate-to-distribute evaluated?

---

# J. Operations / cost

1. Cost per application?
2. Cost per booked loan?
3. Underwriting FTE per 1,000 applications?
4. Which steps consume most time?
5. What is exception/rework rate?
6. What is manual KYB rate?
7. What is document-processing cost?
8. Collections cost by product?
9. What could be automated without changing risk policy?
10. Where does automation currently add complexity instead of removing it?

---

# K. Economics

1. Can you calculate:
   **yield − funding − loss − OPEX − capital** by product?
2. Can you do it by decision model?
3. By existing/new customer?
4. By repeat/first loan?
5. By channel?
6. By guarantee/non-guarantee?
7. Are cross-sell economics included?
8. How are relationship revenues allocated?
9. Which metric drives product decisions today?
10. Which economic line is least reliable?

---

# L. Management / governance

1. Who owns end-to-end SME lending economics?
2. Which KPI can Product optimize independently?
3. Which KPI can Risk optimize independently?
4. Is there a single factory owner?
5. How are policy changes tested?
6. Are pilots randomized / controlled where possible?
7. How quickly can a threshold or rule be changed?
8. Who approves model/rule changes?
9. How are adverse outcomes detected?
10. What is the decision cadence for portfolio reallocation?

---

# Workshop output

For each answer capture:

- fact;
- owner;
- data source;
- confidence;
- pain point;
- target capability;
- value hypothesis;
- dependency.

The workshop should finish with:
1. 3–5 highest-value gaps;
2. data required to validate them;
3. a short list of pilots, not a giant transformation backlog.
